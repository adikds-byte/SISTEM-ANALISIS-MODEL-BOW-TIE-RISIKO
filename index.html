1. STABILITAS LERENG & TSF (Mohr-Coulomb)

Faktor Keamanan lereng tambang & tanggul tailing terhadap tekanan air pori ($u = r_u \cdot \gamma z$):

FoS = [c' + (σn - u)·tan φ'] / [γ·z·sin α·cos α]
Acuan: Kepmen ESDM 1827K/2018 & SNI 8460:2017 (FoS Statis ≥ 1.3–1.5)
2. KUANTIFIKASI BOW-TIE & LOPA

Frekuensi kejadian puncak (*Top Event*) & konsekuensi fatalitas residual:

F_TE = Σ [ f_threat,i × Π (PFD_prev,j) ]
R_fatal = F_TE × Π (PFD_mit,k)
PFD = Probability of Failure on Demand (1 - Efektivitas Barrier)
3. JARAK HENTI HAUL TRUCK (HD)

Jarak pengereman darurat unit HD bermuatan pada jalan angkut menurun (*grade* $\theta$):

d_stop = v·t_r + v² / [2g·(μ·cos θ - sin θ)]
Desain Runaway Ramp / Arrester Bed & Safety Berm (2/3 Roda HD)
4. INVERSE VELOCITY LONGSOR (Fukuzono)

Prediksi waktu keruntuhan lereng ($t_f$) dari radar interferometri SSR:

1 / v(t) = [A · (α - 1)]^(1/(α-1)) · (t_f - t)^(1/(α-1))
Memicu sirine evakuasi TARP Level 4 saat v > 10 mm/jam
